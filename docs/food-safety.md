# Food handling boundaries

PantryFlow compares **recorded quantities**, not the condition of food. A match or a "covered by stock" badge cannot tell whether an ingredient is safe to eat, whether an allergen is present, or whether a suggested substitute suits someone's needs. Check packaging and the actual food, and follow reliable handling guidance. The sample recipes carry no nutrition or dietary suitability score.

The inventory stores two distinct optional fields:

| Field | What it means | What it does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Printed label date | A date the person transcribed from packaging | A safety verdict from the app |
| Your CHECK date | A reminder the person chose to revisit an item | An expiry estimate or permission to eat it |

Neither date is used to rank recipes or predict spoilage. Labels can have different meanings, and storage history matters. See the FDA's [storing food safely](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/are-you-storing-food-safely) guidance.

When a confirmed meal's stock match includes a Freezer entry, the app displays an **optional planning reminder**. It makes no thaw-time estimate. If thawing is needed, follow package directions and the FDA's [safe food handling](https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/safe-food-handling) guidance; do **not** thaw food on the counter. Some products can be prepared from frozen according to their packaging. The cook decides what to use and updates stock manually after cooking.

Any later expiry, defrost or proactive feature would need clear source evidence, user review, testing and a way to express uncertainty before it appears as guidance.
