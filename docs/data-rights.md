# Data rights and provenance

The root [MIT license](../LICENSE) covers PantryFlow's software code and documentation. It **does not grant rights to recipe or product datasets** merely because they appear beside code. The bundled [`data/recipes.json`](../data/recipes.json) contains ten original demonstration records with a [provenance record](../data/PROVENANCE.md); their reuse and redistribution rights are reserved pending a separate, explicit data-license decision by the rightsholder. You may run this starter with its supplied examples. Do not assume the MIT code license permits repackaging the recipe records into another dataset.

No external product data, scraped recipe text, photographs or third-party assets are included. The app does not call a recipe or product API. Future imports should record, per source and record where applicable: creator, origin URL or identifier, collection date, permission or license, attribution requirements, transformations and removal process. Keep imported data in separately identified files with its own terms; let users correct mismatches before saving them. If rights cannot be established, use a link or an original summary rather than copying protected expression.

[Open Food Facts offers an API](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/openfoodfacts-server/api/), but its [terms of use](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use) and the rights of individual fields or images must be reviewed before any integration. [FoodOn](https://github.com/FoodOntology/foodon) is a useful ontology reference, not a pre-cleared content import. [grocy](https://github.com/grocy/grocy) and [Mealie](https://github.com/mealie-recipes/mealie) informed the landscape review only. In particular, no AGPL Mealie code, assets or recipe content were copied into this MIT repository.

For contributions, write original recipe wording or supply evidence that the exact data and assets can be redistributed under clearly stated terms. Document provenance alongside any new catalog rather than relying on a generic repository license.
